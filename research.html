Research

I work on hardware security, focusing on GPUs, DRAM and heterogeneous memory systems.

Projects

PAGE404: GPU isolation on the NVIDIA GH200

Hardware-assisted page migration in GPUs can be abused to break MIG isolation between co-located workloads.

Not so Refreshing: Rowhammer mitigations as attack vectors

The DRAM Refresh Management (RFM) mitigation itself can be exploited to attack GPUs.

NVBleed: Multi-GPU interconnect channels

Contention-based covert and side-channel attacks on the NVIDIA NVLink interconnect.

PRACtical: Efficient Rowhammer defense

Subarray-level counter updates and bank-level recovery isolation reduce the performance overhead of PRAC.
